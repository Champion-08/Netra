Intelligence Loop

  1. IngestRecords stream in from field units, CSV uploads, and the manual report form.
  2. DetectIsolation Forest flags outliers; DBSCAN clusters geographic hotspots automatically.
  3. CorrelateGraph engine links suspects, victims, and locations — exposing hidden networks.
  4. ForecastDistrict risk polygons recalculated on demand; hour-of-day matrices pinpoint timing patterns.
  5. DeployAnalysts export intelligence reports and push actionable briefings to field commanders.
